What brands of clothing do you offer?

Learn how to identify clothing brands Gelato partners with and understand our local production

October 30, 2025
Copy for LLM

This article lists the clothing brands offered by Gelato and explains why the brand of your product might vary.

 

 

Our Clothing Suppliers

We understand that quality is important to you and your customers. That’s why we partner with leading clothing brands known for their consistent and superior quality. Our clothing suppliers vary by country, product, and location to ensure the fastest delivery times and best prices.

 

Because we produce locally, you cannot order products from a specific clothing brand.

Sustainable Practices

Sustainability is at the core of how we make decisions at Gelato. That's why we prioritize local production. This allows us to:

Support local communities of entrepreneurs and business owners.

Significantly reduce delivery distances, which are a major contributor to carbon emissions in the print-on-demand industry.

 

 

Substitute Garments

At Gelato, we strive to keep your preferred garments in stock. However, if a blank garment is out of stock, we may use a substitute garment to prevent delays. Rest assured that we have strict guidelines for garment substitutions:

The substitute garment will be equivalent to the primary garment in terms of style, size, material composition, and color.

We will only use a substitute if the primary garment is out of stock.

The substitute garment will be of equivalent or higher quality compared to the primary garment.

To learn more about the quality and details of our products, browse our product catalog. You can find information about the possible brands and models used for each product.

 

For example, a Performance Unisex Crewneck T-shirt might be a Sport Tek ST350, Gildan 42000, Stedman ST8000, or Just Cool JC001.

 

For more detailed product information, we recommend visiting our suppliers’ websites.
