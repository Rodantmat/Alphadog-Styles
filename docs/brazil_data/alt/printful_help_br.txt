2. Set shipping rates that fit your pricing strategy

For every order you get, Printful charges you for shipping. Shipping costs depend on the amount and type of products in each order, as well as the selected shipping option. Printful offers domestic and international shipping.

You set up your own shipping rates for customers on your storefront. Lower shipping costs help you become competitive in the market and avoid cart abandonment. You can increase the price of your products to cover shipping costs and offer your customers free shipping or set up Printful’s rates as they are among the best in the industry. Read more about developing your shipping pricing strategy.

A few more things to note
Shipping destinations
Printful ships globally, but there are some countries we can’t deliver to due to legal or shipping carrier restrictions.
Customs fees
Depending on your order’s destination and fulfillment location, your customers might have to pay customs fees. The fees vary depending on the order value, country limits, and other factors. Learn more about customs fees.
Live shipping rates
Live rates are calculated by carriers in real-time and displayed during order checkout on your storefront. These rates are determined by the order fulfillment location, shipping destination, shipping method, and carrier pricing. Printful integrations like Shopify, WooCommerce, Ecwid, and Launch Cart support live shipping rates, and you can enable them on your storefront.
Return address
Your store’s return address is Printful’s by default, but you can change it to your own in some cases. Read more about Printful’s Return Policy and return addresses.

We ship more than 1 million items each month, and partner with carriers like USPS, FedEx, DPD, and DHL to give you and your customers the best possible shipping experience.

Our white-label tracking page combines data from these carriers to make it easier for you and your customers to follow packages. You can brand this page with your logo, social media handles, contact info and links to your store.
