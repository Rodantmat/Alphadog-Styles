**Pages fetched: 55** (successful WebFetch calls that returned page content; each logged in section A). Plus 10 WebSearch queries. Failed or skipped fetches are listed at the end of section A and are NOT counted.
**Phrases collected: 393 rows** in section B. Every phrase was seen in a fetched page; none invented. Some rows bundle a short list of terms (e.g. slang glossary) into one row.

**Read this first (honesty limits):**
- I could NOT get actual on-platform TikTok / Instagram / Pinterest / YouTube Shorts post text with like or view counts. TikTok Creative Center was not fetchable, social sites are blocked, Reddit skipped. "Traction evidence" is therefore a PROXY: Etsy sample position, Star Seller / favorites / review counts, how many days a paid Meta ad kept running, search volume. Where a page only gave generic how-to advice, the traction column says "none".
- WebFetch summarises pages with a small model, so verbatim text is as returned by the tool, often truncated at about 125 characters (shown with "..." or as the tool cut it).
- RankHero numbers (monthly searches, competition) are third-party Etsy-keyword estimates. The pages contradict themselves in places (two competition figures, "held steady" next to a 6x swing). Treat as directional. Data window shown on pages is Oct 2025 to Sep 2026; "Etsy sample #n" means the nth of the 12 sample listings the page showed, and the page does not say the order is by sales.
- Source IDs (P1..P55) below map to the URLs in section A.

## D. Concrete wording implications for a dog apparel brand (each tied to evidence above)

1. **Lead product titles with the exact search chain "[Breed] [Mom|Dad|Mama] [Shirt|Sweatshirt|Hoodie]" then a gift word.** Etsy winners (P16-P19, P51-P52) all do this; "dog mama shirt" has the best volume vs competition (P28), "dog mom hoodie" is a low-competition niche (P54). Include "Mama", "Mom" and (for UK) "Mum" variants.
2. **Time the wording to three peaks, not all year:** November-December gift wording, May "Mother's Day" for dog mom, June "Father's Day" for dog dad (June dog dad gift 14,800 vs 1,300 in Jan, P53). Seasonal pun names (P5) and "Christmas Gift for Dog Dad" style titles (P17) match this.
3. **Use proven humour templates but put the breed name in the slot.** "I Got That Dog In Me, [Breed]" worked across 3 breeds (P29); "Treat Dealer", "Dog Hair Don't Care", "Sorry I'm Late I Saw A Dog", "Dog Mom Era", "Stay Golden / Life is Golden", "Coffee and [Breed]s" recur across shops. Because they are crowded, differentiate through the breed plus a distinct second line, not by copying the generic one.
4. **Offer personalisation wording** ("Custom ... with Pet Name on Sleeve", "gotcha day year", "Dogs Names On The Sleeve, Puff Lettering"; P2, P18, P54). 22 tagged rows, and personalised pet-face ads were the longest-running ad type in P33.
5. **For short-form video hooks, adapt the templates that recur across the hook lists:** "POV: [dog scenario]" (P12, P13), "If you're a [dog mom/dad], you need to see this" (P42), "Would you wear this?" with a charity line (P34, 391 days), "Tag a [dog mom] who gets it" (P45), "Only real [breed] owners get this" (P45 pattern), "Day 1 vs day 30" does not suit apparel; "Packing orders for my small business" and "Pack an Order with Me" (P14, P42) do suit a small shop.
6. **Put the caption in frame one and make it identity-based.** P42 reports about 70% of top ads had a caption in frame one; P45 reports 65% watch muted and 15-30 s POD videos. These are aggregate claims, not verified per-hook data; treat as test hypotheses.
7. **Consider a "each purchase does X" line only if the business can truly deliver it.** It is the single longest-running angle in the ad libraries (P34, 391-1,100 days), but it is a commitment, not just copy.
8. **Dog-voice captions and DoggoLingo are safe supporting texture, not the main hook.** DoggoLingo has real history (doggo 2014, KYM entry, 3.7M-view explainer; P41) and breed nicknames (pibble, shibe, shoob, weenie, floofer). The caption-list pages that use it have no traction data, so keep it as secondary text.
9. **Avoid generic sentimental captions as hooks.** "Dogs: the ultimate loyalty experts" style lines (P47) and "Capturing life's simple joys, one wagging tail at a time" (P38) appear only in caption-generator pages, never in a merch title or ad with run-length evidence.
10. **Gaps to fill before committing:** no per-post likes/views were captured for TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest or Shorts, and Pinterest Trends showed no pet terms (P15, P50). Next step would be a browser-based pass on TikTok Creative Center and Pinterest Trends, plus RankHero keywords for dachshund/frenchie/corgi/pitbull (these pages were not on the allowlist here).
