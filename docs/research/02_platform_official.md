## (b) Consolidated findings

### Cross-platform (what at least 3 official sources agree on)
1. **Hook window is 1-6 s, mostly asserted not proven.** TikTok: first 3-6 s (#25, #28, #54), "90% of ad recall within 6 s" (#23, 2020 data), value prop in first 3 s (#24). Instagram: first 3 s and track View Rate (#10, #46). Pinterest: first 6 s, logo/product in first 1-2 s (#32, #33), takeaway within 10 s (#35). YouTube: "one second" (#17), first few seconds (#18, #49). Consequence for a 10 s slideshow: frame 1 must carry the message.
2. **Sound-off design is endorsed everywhere**: TikTok (#22, #27), Pinterest (#2, #33), YouTube (#18), Google Ads (#16). Counter-evidence: Meta says >75% of Reels views are sound-on (#30) and TikTok says 90% call sound vital (#26). Resolve: do both, music present AND text carries meaning.
3. **Text overlay should be minimal and single-message** (#59 one image one message; #34 clarity over cleverness; #32 readable on small screens). Only one number exists: TikTok "about 5-10 words per second" (#24), i.e. roughly 5-10 words per second means short lines on a slideshow.
4. **Keywords in text matter for search on every platform**: Instagram Search uses caption, bio, hashtags, content (#6); Pinterest titles/descriptions/board/overlay (#11, #13, #14, #20); YouTube 1-2 main keywords in title and description, first lines (#47); TikTok uses captions, sounds, hashtags as ranking inputs (#43) and seller guidance says use product keywords (#56). Only Pinterest/YouTube give mechanism detail; TikTok SEO claims from official sources are thin (official TikTok search pages did not load).
5. **CTA**: explicit, action-verb, tied to a specific outcome or occasion (#25, #34, #54, #27). Pinterest says avoid vague "Learn more" when the next step is purchase (#34). TikTok: put long CTAs in caption not audio (#29); avoid "Swipe Up" (#59).
6. **Save/share/comment prompts**: Pinterest explicitly says asking for saves/follows raises visibility (#2). Instagram lists shares, saves, comments and speed (#6, #46) as signals but ALSO lists engagement bait as reach-limiting (#10). The line between a genuine prompt and bait is not defined; avoid "like/comment/share to win" and contest phrasing.
7. **Originality and duplication are penalised** (Instagram #3, #10; Facebook #31; Pinterest #11; TikTok #43 no duplicates). Slideshows reusing identical photos + identical caption across accounts risk this. Facebook: copies of a video get reduced distribution and the original gets visibility (#31).

### TikTok
- Recommendation inputs: interactions; video info (captions, sounds, hashtags); device/account; follower count not a direct factor (#43, 2020).
- Hashtags: only relevant ones; #FYP does not help (#55); mix campaign/product/community/trending (#56). No official count.
- Shop policy: whole-video still/looping image content is not "high quality" unless face and physical product are present (#44). A pure slideshow of product photos is exactly the format flagged; relevant if TikTok Shop is used, and a possible hint of how organic quality is judged (inference, not stated).
- Hook types with official templates: popularity, problem/tip, price, audience call-out, "3 reasons" list (#25, #28); negative hooks mentioned (#28).
- Evidence quality: nearly all numbers are TikTok-commissioned ad studies (Lumen, Kantar, Marketcast) 2020-2023; they concern ads, not organic.

### Instagram / Facebook Reels
- Reels signals: watch to end, reshare, like, "funny/entertaining" rating, audio page visits (#5, #7); engagement per viewer incl. watch time, shares, follows, profile visits (#10); reach tested on small non-follower audience first (#46).
- Reels **not recommended** if low-res, watermarked, muted, bordered, or **majority-text** (#7, 2021, verbatim list). Relevance: coloured-background slideshows must not be mostly text; keep audio on; no visible TikTok watermark; avoid borders/letterboxing.
- Hashtags: irrelevant hurt (#10); Facebook: <=5 hashtags, no links in caption, minimal capitalisation (#31).
- Keywords in captions help Instagram Search (#6).
- Auto captions transcribe speech only (#8); a silent slideshow gets nothing from them, so text must be burned in.
- Early 30-min engagement does not boost ranking (#10), so launch-hour comment pumping is pointless per Instagram.
- Posting volume: top net-follower-growth accounts ~10+ Reels/month (#10, correlation).

### Pinterest
- Distribution: engagement (saves, follows), context, quality, originality, keywords in titles/descriptions/boards (#11). Pins can rank months later.
- Title: first 40 chars show in feed, max 100 (#37); descriptions up to 800 but not shown in the feed (#37), still searchable (#11). Official Oct 2026 tip: product type + defining feature + occasion, plain shopper language (#13).
- Video: 6-15 s recommended (#33, #37); 9:16; logo/product in first 1-2 s (#32); use text overlay instead of title cards (#35); leave space top/bottom (#35); strong cover/thumbnail (#1, #2).
- Trends: Predicts 2026 is fashion/beauty/food led, no pet mentions found (#38-#39, #53); Trends tool free, compare 4 keywords (#51). Pinterest's own results claims are brand-lift studies (B).
- Up to 10 hidden topic tags (#35).

### YouTube Shorts
- Title max 100 chars (#42); description first lines should describe the video, 1-2 key words (#47); up to 3 hashtags display (#48); >60 voids all (#48).
- Metric: "viewed vs swiped away" shows hook success (#41); as of 2026-08-24 views start at first frame, so raw view counts inflate and engaged views are the real signal (#40).
- Interactive stickers and comment video-replies are official engagement levers (#19).
- "Get feedback" analyses hook and pacing (#42).

### What official sources do NOT say (gaps)
- No official numeric weights for any signal.
- No official figure for how much text-overlay hooks lift hook rate; only qualitative.
- No official evidence on question vs statement vs number hooks (TikTok lists formats only).
- No official data on emoji, caption length, or comment-pinning effects.
- No official dog/pet vertical data found (Predicts 2026 omits pets).
- TikTok Creator Academy and Support pages were not retrievable, so TikTok search/SEO official wording is unverified here.

## (c) Concrete wording implications for a dog-apparel brand (10 s slideshow reels)

Items marked [official] follow directly from a source above; [inference] is my extrapolation.

1. **Frame 1 carries the whole promise.** [official: #24, #32, #46] Put the hook text on slide 1 (breed + product + occasion), product visible at once, no logo-only opener. Example: "Frenchie mom gift: new line-art hoodie".
2. **Hook templates endorsed by TikTok** [official: #25, #28, #54]: audience call-out ("Corgi owners, this is for you"), listicle ("3 breeds just added"), occasion ("The perfect gift for a Lab dad"), popularity ("Everyone with a Dachshund wants this"), price/promo if real. Test negative hooks ("Stop buying boring dog shirts") per #28, sparingly [inference on suitability].
3. **One message per slide, about 5-10 words per second at most; realistically 3-6 words per slide** [official: #24, #59]. Big, readable, inside the safe zone and away from top/bottom UI [official: #30, #35, #59]. 
4. **Text must carry the meaning without sound AND a track must be present** [official: #2, #33 vs #7, #30].
5. **Do not make the reel mostly text** [official: #7 "majority-text" excluded from Instagram Reel recommendations]. Keep photos dominant, text as a short overlay, no borders.
6. **Burn the keywords into overlay and caption**: breed + garment + occasion ("golden retriever hoodie", "dog dad gift") [official: #6, #11, #13, #47]. Pinterest title formula: product type + defining feature + occasion (#13): "Golden Retriever Line Art Hoodie - Gift for Dog Moms". Keep the first 40 characters decisive (#37). YouTube: lead with 1-2 main keywords and describe the video in the first lines (#47).
7. **Descriptive, not cute, titles on Pinterest/YouTube**, plain shopper language, not internal collection names [official: #13]. Save the personality for the on-screen hook.
8. **CTA wording**: action verb plus specific outcome plus occasion: "Shop the Beagle hoodie - link in bio", "Gift idea for Mother's Day" [official: #25, #34, #54]. Avoid vague "Learn more" [#34]. Put the long CTA in the caption, keep the on-screen one to 2-4 words [#29]. No "swipe up" [#59].
9. **Engagement prompts**: Pinterest-safe "Save for later / save this gift idea" is endorsed [#2]. Instagram: avoid explicit like/comment/share bait and giveaways [#10]. A natural question ("Which breed next?") feeds comment replies and YouTube comment video-replies [#19] [inference that it counts as organic, not bait].
10. **Hashtags**: few and relevant. Facebook <=5 [#31]; YouTube shows up to 3 [#48]; irrelevant ones hurt on Instagram [#10]; #FYP useless on TikTok [#55]. Use breed + gift + product tags, no stuffing.
11. **No links in Facebook captions** [#31]; put link in bio or comments.
12. **Vary caption and overlay text across accounts and posts** [official: #3, #10, #11, #31]; duplicate content is demoted, especially cross-posted copies with identical copy [inference for text-only duplication].
13. **Seasonal/occasion planning**: Pinterest says plan weeks to months ahead and use rising search terms in titles/descriptions [#39, #52]. Dog-gift occasions (Mother's/Father's Day, Christmas, National Dog Day) fit [inference].
14. **Slideshow policy risk on TikTok Shop**: static/looping image videos not accepted as high quality without face + product [#44]. Add motion (zoom, transitions) for at least 3 s [#44] and consider a human wearing the item.
15. **Measure with the platforms' own hook metrics**: Instagram View Rate (#46), YouTube viewed-vs-swiped (#41), Pinterest saves (#11). Do not trust the early-engagement window on Instagram (#10).
16. **Testing cadence**: TikTok suggests 3-5 creatives per group, refresh weekly (#24, #28, #54). Test hook text A/B with the same visuals [inference].

### Reliability summary
Everything above that carries a percentage comes from platform-commissioned or internal studies, mostly for ads and mostly 2020-2023; treat as directional. The strongest, least biased items are hard specs and policies: Pinterest title limits (#37), Instagram non-recommendation list (#7), YouTube hashtag rules (#48), Facebook caption advice (#31), TikTok Shop still-image rule (#44).
