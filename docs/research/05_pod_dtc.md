## (b) Consolidated findings

Reliability first. No fetched page contains a controlled test of one phrase against another for a POD/dog apparel brand. What exists falls in four tiers:
1. Hard data, not about wording per se: Rival IQ (pet brands, 2023 data), Tailwind (1.2M pins), NewsWhip (pet posts, 2016), Socialinsider (Gymshark 2025), Virlo (survivor hooks with views).
2. Platform rules from Etsy's own handbook (high reliability, rule-level).
3. Named real brand copy without metrics: Wild One captions/ads, BarkBox squirrel posts, Printify content-calendar brand examples, Camping With Dogs.
4. Vendor guides with illustrative hooks and uncited statistics (Printify, Gelato, Merch Titans, Mega Digital, Top Growth). Treat their numbers (5-8% conversion, 99% less competition, +200% sales) as unverified.

What repeatedly appears across independent sources (convergence = better evidence than any single claim):
- Hook within 1-3 s; product visible within ~2 s; 7-15 s is the recommended POD length (Printify x3, Gelato, Printful, Merch Titans). Matches the 10 s slideshow format.
- Gift framing and a named recipient identity ("for the dog mom who...", "POV: your friend is a...") is the dominant POD hook family (Gelato, Merch Titans, Printful, Etsy handbook tag categories "who it's for", "occasions").
- Breed-/identity-specific wording beats generic ("Golden Retriever Mom" vs "Dog Mom") per Merch Titans x2 and Printify; numbers are uncited, but Etsy handbook's long-tail advice and Growing Your Craft agree directionally.
- Engagement-question captions and "which one" prompts (Gelato, Printify, Popflex, Socialinsider).
- Social proof/scarcity phrasing from real brands: "has sold out a few times", "get yours while you still can", "This hoodie sold out twice already" (Printify calendar). Only usable if true.
- Honest, personified dog humor is the proven pet-brand voice: BarkBox ("Dogs are silly... so the content should be, too"), Chewy "what your pet is thinking" captions, BarkBox squirrel takeover. Rival IQ shows memes and UGC top pet-brand engagement (Spot & Tango 4.33% IG vs 0.33% median; BarkBox UGC reel 12.7M impressions).
- Pet content lifts comments more than likes (NewsWhip: +89% comments average vs +19% likes), so comment-bait captions fit the medium.
- Captions that ask viewers to stay to the end of the carousel/video were used by BarkBox to boost reach (Rival IQ).
- Pinterest/Etsy are search engines: keyword-first specific titles, 2-3 word phrases, short focused descriptions (220-232 chars avg for viral pins), alt text (+123% outbound clicks), vertical 2:3. Tailwind and Etsy agree.
- Etsy: item name first; subjective words in description; no pipe-stuffing; 13 multi-word tags that someone would type.
- Generic CTAs are weak per agency (uncited) but real CTAs in POD guides are plain: "Link in bio", "Tap the Shop tab", "Search [store]", "Grab the matching mug too", "Tag someone who needs this".
- On-screen text: product-forward first frame, change shot or text every 2-3 s (Printful), test text vs no text (Printify), keep text short.
- Realism: most merchants' posts reach only low hundreds of views (Printful mistakes). Expect volume and format iteration (80/20: keep 80% of a proven format, change 20%) rather than one magic phrase. Printify suggests testing >=3 hooks per product and about 20 videos in 2 weeks.
- Seasonal: Father's/Mother's Day gift campaigns start 4-6 weeks ahead (Merchize).

Gaps: nothing found on Facebook caption wording for POD, nothing quantifying hook-text vs caption-text effect, and no dog-apparel-specific brand teardown with actual post metrics.

## (c) Concrete wording implications for a dog apparel brand (30+ phrases)

Hooks / on-screen text (first 2 s of the 10 s reel). Evidence in brackets; "untested" = vendor-suggested, no data.
1. "POV: your dog's face is now your favorite hoodie" - POV format, Printify calendar ("POV: Your new favorite hoodie just arrived"); untested.
2. "For the Golden Retriever mom who has everything (except this)" - identity + breed specificity (Merch Titans; Etsy "who it's for").
3. "Stop scrolling if you're a Corgi dad" - Printify/ecommercefastlane "Stop scrolling if..." hook; untested.
4. "Be honest, does the real hoodie look better than the mockup?" - Printify calendar Day 10; works only with real photos.
5. "The only shirt my husband will wear since we got the Lab" - adapted from Gelato "The only shirt my dad will wear now."
6. "My dog asked for this" - personification/"what your pet is thinking" (Chewy #ChewyChattyPets, BarkBox voice).
7. "He thinks he designed it." - dog-as-author humor in BarkBox's squirrel/mascot tradition; untested.
8. "The perfect gift for the dog dad who says he wants nothing" - Gelato Father's Day hook, direct adaptation.
9. "POV: you finally found a gift for your dog-obsessed best friend" - Gelato graduation hook adapted.
10. "Can't find a hoodie that shows your breed right? Same..." - Printify calendar problem-to-product ("Can't find a tee that actually fits right? Same...").
11. "I couldn't find a Dachshund hoodie I liked, so I made one" - "I couldn't find it, so I made it" (Printify, Printful guides).
12. "Don't show this to a dog mom..." - adapted from the 62.5M-view "DONT show this to a boy mom..." (Virlo); high risk of tonal mismatch, test small.
13. "If you have a Frenchie... you need this." - adapted from "If you wear glasses... you need these." (23.4M views, Virlo).
14. "If your boyfriend's dog hair is on everything, just get him this" - adapted from "If your boyfriend's car center console looks like a literal trash can, just order him this." (16.3M, Virlo).
15. "You keep asking about the Corgi one, so here it is" - Printify calendar Day 24 FAQ hook; true only if people ask.
16. "The question I get asked most about this hoodie..." - Printify calendar Day 16.
17. "Check out what my mom just bought me" - Printful's concrete hook upgrade; for dogs: "Look what my dog's mom just bought him".

Product titles (Etsy/Pinterest/Merch) - rules from Etsy handbook, Tailwind, Merch Informer:
18. "Golden Retriever Mom Hoodie - Line Art Dog Breed Sweatshirt, Gift for Dog Lover" - item first, breed, audience, gift (Etsy handbook; Printify etsy-listings titles).
19. "French Bulldog Dad Hat - Minimalist Line Art Frenchie Cap, Unisex" - same pattern; Merch Informer title checklist (keyword, design, audience, product type).
20. "Dachshund Line Art T-Shirt, Funny Gift for Doxie Owners" - Etsy tag test "would someone type it".
21. "Dog Breed Line Art Hoodie, Gift for New Puppy Owner" - Growing Your Craft "gift for new puppy owner".
22. Avoid "Cute shirt"; avoid pipe lists "Dog Mom | Dog Dad | Dog Lover | ..." (Pingenerator, Printful, Etsy handbook).
