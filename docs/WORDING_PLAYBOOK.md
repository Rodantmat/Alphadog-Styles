# AlphaDog Styles wording playbook (research of 2026-11-10)
Basis: 10 researchers, 569 pages actually fetched, 20 source types (full logs in docs/research/01-10). Evidence is mostly correlational or vendor-run; almost nothing tests on-screen wording for product reels directly. Everything below is a HYPOTHESIS to A/B test on our own account, ranked by evidence strength. Never fabricate stats; never copy others' slogans (only their patterns).

## 1. On-screen text spec (best-supported, from reading-speed and accessibility research)
- Total text per 10 s reel: <= 25 words; reading pace <= 2.5 words/s (target 2). Each card held >= 1.2 s (2 s for the hook). Replace cards, never scroll them.
- Hook: 3-5 words, visible from frame 1, held 1.5-2 s, 60-80 px bold sans; body text >= 48 px; max 2 lines of ~20-28 chars.
- Contrast >= 4.5:1 (white + dark outline, or box <= 75% opacity). Text never over merch (owner rule). Do not duplicate caption word-for-word; reword.
- Safe zones for Reels/TikTok: top 14%, bottom 35%, sides 6% are covered by UI. Hook at y 300-700, CTA at y 1250-1450 (of 1920). Keep logo small; big logo + subtitles lowers recall.
- Instagram does NOT recommend: low-res, watermarked, muted, bordered, majority-text reels. Auto-captions only transcribe speech, so a silent reel needs burned-in text, but keep it a minority of the frame.
- TikTok Shop (Oct 2026 policy): rejects videos made only of still/looping images unless a face + the physical product appear and >= 3 s motion. People-wearing-merch frames + motion satisfy this; check before using Shop.

## 2. Hook wording (evidence in brackets)
1. Identity/breed self-portrait (Rover 2024: 71% say breed reflects personality; owners wear dog-mom apparel 40%): "Your breed. Your hoodie." / "Golden dad energy." / "Is this your dog?"
2. Scenario/POV beats questions in the largest hook dataset found (2,421 TikToks): "POV: your dog has merch", "POV: you found your breed's hoodie".
3. Specific beats generic (vidIQ 4M+ titles: "Turning X into Y", "Caught on camera" 3-4x; trailing question 0.82x): "Boxer line art, now on a hoodie".
4. Humour / dog voice with a human wink (BarkBox style; Rival IQ: memes+UGC top engagement): "Sorry I'm late, I saw a dog."
5. Second person ("you/your") early: TikTok ad report +128% purchase intent, but randomised headline tests show tiny/negative effect -> use as one variant, not the rule.
6. Emotion: high-arousal positive (amusement, awe) drives sharing; sadness and guilt lower it (Berger & Milkman). Pay off the hook inside 10 s: clickbait without payoff -48% shares.
7. Gift occasions (Dec peak "dog lover gift" 40.5k searches, "dog mom" May, "dog dad gift" June): "The easiest gift for a dog person."
8. Personalization hooks (gotcha-day, dog's name): high frequency in Etsy bestsellers.
Avoid: all caps on long lines, sad framing, fake scarcity, "like/follow" asks (likes asks -60% likes on TikTok), engagement bait.

## 2b. CTA
- One CTA, 3-6 words, specific verb, in the last 2-2.5 s, after the payoff. Reason to send beats "tag 3": "Send this to the dog person in your life" / "Save for your next gift".
- Caption question lifts comments 26-37% (Metricool, 24M IG + 2.3M TikTok posts); comment-CTA +14% TikTok, +203% IG. Ecommerce sample showed no sales difference: treat as engagement, not sales.
- "Shop Now" > "Learn More" (weak evidence). Add 'link in bio'.

## 3. Captions and hashtags
- Front-load the first 125 characters (fold). TikTok: under 30 words beat 90+ (4.9% vs 3.9%, 9.7M videos); 101-200 characters is a common sweet spot. Facebook: no links, <= 5 hashtags. IG 3-4 hashtags; TikTok 1-5 specific, no #fyp; YouTube <= 3. Emoji 0-3 (neutral).
- Pinterest (strongest text platform): title keyword-first, first 40 chars visible: "<product type> + <defining feature> + <occasion>", e.g. "Boxer Line Art Hoodie, Dog Dad Gift". Description ~220 chars, <= 5 related keywords. Always add alt text. Vertical 2:3 or taller.
- Etsy-style title pattern (for store listings): "[Breed] Mom/Dad Hoodie, ... Gift"; subjective words in description.
- Reply to comments (394 dog accounts: replying to most comments 1.99% vs 1.33% engagement; first comment from creator +44%; correlational).

## 4. Voice and breed strategy
- Women: "dog mom"; men: "dog dad" + humour/breed pride (52% of women vs 26% of men say parent). Mixed/rescue dogs = big unaddressed group.
- Breed-as-person format ("If dog breeds were people") reached 40M+ views as a numbered series. Build a series per breed instead of one-offs.
- Recurring slogan TEMPLATES in the market (patterns only, do not copy text): "I got that dog in me", "[breed] mom era", "treat dealer", "sorry I'm late I saw a dog", "stay golden".

## 5. Testing protocol (everything above is a hypothesis)
- 3-5 hook variants per design; change ONE variable at a time (hook, CTA, text on/off); keep 80% of a winner, change 20%. Compare per platform at 24 h and 72 h using the weekly review. Most variants fail (about 3.5% of ads are outliers), so judge on volume.
- Variants: V0 no text, V1 hook only, V2 hook + CTA, V3 identity hook, V4 POV hook.

## 6. Known gaps
No dataset tests on-screen wording on product slideshows; no pet-apparel hook A/B data; Reddit/forum evidence unavailable; TikTok/IG on-platform counts for competitors unavailable.
